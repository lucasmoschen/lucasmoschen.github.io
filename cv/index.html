General Information

Name Lucas M. Moschen
Languages Portuguese, English, studying French
E-mail lucas.moschen22@imperial.ac.uk
Description My research combines mathematical modelling, control theory, and numerical analysis to study probability distributions and interacting stochastic systems. I develop feedback control methods for Fokker-Planck, McKean-Vlasov, and Wasserstein gradient-flow models based on spectral properties, with current work on coupled populations, sampling, and effective dynamics. My earlier research concerns constrained vaccination policies on networks and statistical inference for correlated proportions.
