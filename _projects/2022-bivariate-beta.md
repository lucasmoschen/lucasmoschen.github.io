With Luiz Max Carvalho, I study statistical models for pairs of correlated proportions. Starting from a bivariate beta distribution, this project develops classical and Bayesian estimation methods, prior elicitation, and diagnostics for model adequacy. It also compares beta and logistic-normal models using simulations and data on childhood vaccination coverage and diagnostic-test sensitivity and specificity.

The resulting paper, [Modelling correlated proportion data: Bayesian and classical approaches to inference and diagnostics](https://hdl.handle.net/10044/1/132384), was published in **TEST (2026)**; the link provides the accepted manuscript. [Bivariate beta distribution: parameter inference and diagnostics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.01271) is an earlier version.
