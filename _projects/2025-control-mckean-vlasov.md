I study how feedback can steer interacting populations towards a desired collective state. This project extends mean-field control methods to systems with several species, including situations where only one population can be controlled, and to dynamics on unbounded domains. The aim is to understand how interactions, available controls, and the geometry of the state space affect stabilisation.
